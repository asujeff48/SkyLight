Sky Above

An interactive night-sky map for wherever you are — or any place you pick.

Launch Sky Above All FunApps

Free to use · Just for fun

Sky Above draws the sky you would see from your viewpoint: planets, bright stars, the Moon (with phase), and the Sun. Use your location, look up a city, scrub the date and time, or watch the last few hours of motion — including ISS passes when they sweep overhead.

It is a playground for looking up, not a professional observatory tool. No account. No fee. Open it, poke around, and enjoy the view.

  • Live sky dome with planets, stars, Moon, and Sun
  • Near me, city search, and time travel controls
  • Moon phase label, filters, and ISS pass replay

Project nickname on GitHub: SkyLight. The app you use is called Sky Above.
