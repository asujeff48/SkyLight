Bigfoot

A trail-cam style map of reported Bigfoot sightings across the contiguous U.S.

Launch Bigfoot All FunApps

Free to use · Just for fun

Bigfoot maps published sighting metadata from the public BFRO geographic database — where, when, and whether it was a visual report or sounds / tracks. Full write-ups stay on BFRO; this app is a playful way to browse the pattern on a map.

Pure entertainment and curiosity. Free to open, free to poke around, no account required.

  • Infrared trail-cam map with walking Bigfoot markers
  • Timeframes: recently posted, this year, or all time
  • Click a silhouette for location, date, and report type
